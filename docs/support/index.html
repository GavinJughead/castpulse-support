CastPulse Support

Questions, problems or privacy requests? Email hellojiawen123@gmail.com. Include your CastPulse version, iPhone model, iOS version and what happened if helpful. Do not send passwords, Apple verification codes, payment details, exact fishing locations or unredacted screenshots.

Record a fishing session

Tap Start Fishing to start timing. Record a hook as landed, lost or pending. Pause for breaks and resume when you return. Hold the finish control and confirm to save. Core recording and history work offline without signing in or purchasing Pro. The Lock Screen status is read-only; unlock and open CastPulse to record or change anything.

Restore a Pro purchase

On the home screen, open Pro Insights → Explore CastPulse Pro → Restore Purchase. Use the same App Store account that originally bought Pro and connect to the internet. Restoring does not charge again, and does not require Sign in with Apple inside CastPulse. Apple handles billing and refunds.

Change the app language

Open More → Field Mode → App Language and choose English or Simplified Chinese.

Back up or remove your data

Open More → Data Management to turn on automatic private iCloud backup or use Back Up Now. Backup is off by default and uses the iCloud account on your iPhone, not the optional Sign in with Apple inside CastPulse. An empty installation using the same iCloud account can offer a restore if a backup is available.

Use More → Data Management to clear your data. If signed in, use More → Account → Delete Account & On-Device Data and confirm. These actions also request deletion of the CastPulse private iCloud backup; cloud deletion may need to retry when iCloud or the network is unavailable. Apple-managed purchase rights are not deleted or refunded. Your iPhone’s system backups and Apple authorization are managed separately in iOS Settings.

CastPulse Privacy Policy

CastPulse 使用支持

如有使用问题、故障或隐私请求，请发邮件到 hellojiawen123@gmail.com。为便于定位问题，可提供 CastPulse 版本、iPhone 型号、iOS 版本和触发步骤。请勿发送密码、Apple 验证码、付款资料、具体钓点或包含个人信息的未打码截图。

记录一场钓鱼

点“开始钓鱼”即开始计时，上钩后可记为成功、脱钩或待定。休息时暂停，回来后继续；长按结束并确认保存。核心记录和历史可离线使用，无需登录或购买 Pro。锁屏状态只读，须解锁并打开 CastPulse 才能操作。

恢复 Pro 购买

在首页依次打开“专业复盘 → 了解 CastPulse Pro → 恢复购买”。连接网络，使用原来购买 Pro 的 App Store 账号。恢复不重复收费，也不需要在 CastPulse 内通过 Apple 登录。付款和退款由 Apple 处理。

更改应用语言

打开“更多 → 现场模式 → App 语言”，选择 English 或简体中文。

备份或删除资料

可在“更多 → 数据管理”开启私人 iCloud 自动备份，或点“立即备份”。默认关闭，使用 iPhone 的 iCloud 账号，与 App 内可选的 Apple 登录相互独立。使用同一 iCloud 账号的空白安装如发现备份，可以提示恢复。

可在“更多 → 数据管理”清除资料；已通过 Apple 登录时也可进入“更多 → 账户 → 删除账号与本机数据”并确认。这些操作也会发起删除 CastPulse 私人 iCloud 备份的请求；iCloud 或网络不可用时可能等待重试。Apple 管理的购买权益不会被删除或退款；系统设备备份和 Apple 授权请在 iOS 设置中单独管理。
